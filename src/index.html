    How to play

    1. Read the prompt during the 3-second preview.
    2. When the field opens, you have 25 seconds to find a title that fits. Keep guessing until you get one or time runs out. English, romaji, native titles, and AniList-listed synonyms work when AniList returns them.
    3. Pick a deep cut. A correct answer earns 10 to 100 points based on how many people list it on AniList.

    Eligible entries include released TV series, films, and streaming series listed with at least two episodes. Tag prompts use AniList tags with a relevance of 60 or more and exclude spoiler tags.

    The daily resets at midnight UTC. Practice has the same format and can be played anytime.
